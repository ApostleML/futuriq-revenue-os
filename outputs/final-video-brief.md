## 1. Who This Is For (one buyer)

**Independent coaches and consultants who sell their time** (1:1 or cohort), have real expertise, and have no written, productized offer.

Creators and other entrepreneurs can use the kit, but the title, hook and CTA never address them. Say it once, early, on camera:
> "This is for coaches and consultants who sell their time. If that's you, stay."

## 2. The Pain

Not "overwhelm." This:

**You've already used AI to make a lead magnet, some content and a few emails. Each piece was fine. Put them side by side and they describe three different businesses, so a visitor who likes you still can't tell what you sell.** A visitor who doesn't understand your offer doesn't join your list.

## 3. Positioning

> I help independent coaches and consultants turn the service they already sell into **one clear offer**, with content, a lead magnet and follow-up that all say the same thing, by installing a four-agent **Revenue Chain** that passes one offer sentence down the line.

Promise: **one sitting, one idea in, a first-draft go-to-market kit out.** It is a draft to edit, not a launched business, and there is no income claim anywhere.

## 4. The Mechanism: The Revenue Chain and the Offer-Sentence Contract

| Order | Agent | Job | Contract |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | market-signal-researcher | Demand, buyer psychology, opportunity score | Research only. Labels inference |
| 2 | offer-architect | Positioning and offer | **Must produce ONE offer sentence** |
| 3 | content-angle-strategist | Title, hook, angles, retention map | **Must quote the offer sentence word for word; flags it if missing** |
| 4 | conversion-system-builder | Lead magnet, CTA, 5 emails | **Must quote the offer sentence word for word; flags it if missing** |
| — | Coordinator | Runs the order, rejects weak output, assembles the kit | Rejects any output that drops the sentence |

**Why one long prompt can't do this:** a single prompt has no hand-off you can inspect, so drift is invisible and you can't tell which step broke. In the chain, you search for the sentence. If it's missing, you know exactly which agent dropped it and you fix that one file.

On screen, show agent 3's "quote the offer sentence" instruction for 5 seconds. That turns the mechanism from a claim into a rule viewers can see.
