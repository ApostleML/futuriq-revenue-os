## Buyer and Pain

### Who this is for
Solo coaches and consultants who sell their own expertise and want leads from YouTube.

They already have a service they can deliver. They have used ChatGPT or Claude in a chat window. They have watched enough "AI tools" videos to distrust the next one. They half-believe the terminal is not for them.

Not for: people with no deliverable service. People who want a push-button or passive-income promise. Mac users (the setup guide is Windows only).

### The trigger
An idea in a notes app.

No offer. No video angle. No lead magnet. No follow-up. Four assets missing, and the idea sits where it was written.

Some of these buyers have tried to fill the gap. They have a chat for the offer, another for the title, a third for the emails. Four documents that do not agree with each other. The offer says one thing. The video promises another. The lead magnet answers a third question. The emails sound like a different business.

### The honest cost of inaction
- The idea stays parked. Nothing exists to point a video, a call or a DM at.
- Or the work gets done in pieces, in separate tools, and lives in disconnected documents that contradict each other.
- A video with no offer behind it sends viewers nowhere. The first video you publish is the first time this matters.

No income figure attached. No claim about what waiting costs in dollars. The cost is a stalled idea and inconsistent assets, and the viewer can verify both in their own notes app.

### Why now (buyer side)
Nothing here expires. There is no deadline and the video must not invent one.

The reason to start is on the buyer's side:
1. The idea exists today. The four missing assets are the only thing between it and a first video.
2. The folder is the exact one on screen. The cost of trying is one working session on one idea, not a rebuild.
3. The first run produces a first draft to edit, which is a better starting point than a blank page and four tabs.

The price of a strategist and a copywriter is not stated anywhere in this video. It is not verified.

## The Mechanism: the Revenue Chain

Sold as four benefits the viewer would say out loud. The architecture sits behind each one.

**The structure.** One idea goes through four specialists, in order. Each has one job. Each works from the written output of the one before. The coordinator is the main Claude Code session. It is not a fifth agent. There are four specialists and no fifth.

A subagent is a role file: a short text document that says "you are the specialist who only does X." One line, said once, folder shown once.

| What the viewer says | What does it | Shown on screen |
|---|---|---|
| "My offer, video, magnet and emails all say the same promise." | **One promise, four places.** The Offer-Sentence Contract. Agent 2 locks the sentence once. Agents 3 and 4 must quote it unchanged, or stop and flag it. | The hook, an angle, the lead magnet promise and Email 1, outlined in one colour, side by side. |
| "It tells me what it guessed." | **Evidence labels.** The researcher marks claims as public evidence, brief, or inference. Later agents carry the label forward and do not upgrade a guess to a fact. | One real `[INFERENCE]` line in the Agent 1 output, then the same claim arriving downstream still marked as a hypothesis. |
| "The research step can't overwrite my files." | **Read-only researcher.** Agent 1 has Write and Edit disabled. It reports back and the main session saves its brief. | The agent file with write access off. Said in one sentence. |
| "If the offer is wrong, I fix one stage and rerun the rest." | **Written handoffs.** Each stage is a file you can open. A bad offer is an Agent 2 fix, then reruns of 3 and 4. | Open any stage's output file. No claim of a quality gain over one prompt. |

What "one promise, four places" prevents: the offer, the video, the magnet and the emails contradicting each other. That is the pain named in the hook and shown at 0:25, resolved at the contract reveal.

### Why it holds up
Five things are on screen. None needs an income claim.
1. A named, narrow demo idea, visible in the first minute.
2. The real `.claude/agents/` folder, four files, opened once.
3. A timer visible the whole video. Elapsed time is read from it and nowhere else.
4. Real output, read aloud: a risk, the offer, the hook, the magnet promise, Email 1.
5. A folder that matches the download file for file.

If the output reads generic, the claim collapses. The fix is a different idea, not softer wording.

## Sales Path

The path starts from what a viewer did, not from urgency. Every rung extends the same Revenue Chain, and each is the next useful step from the one before.

| Rung | What it is | Moves up when the viewer... |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Video (free) | One idea through the chain, the output on screen, the contract reveal. One CTA: the folder. | ...downloads the folder. |
| 2. Starter folder (free, email) | Their own idea through the same chain, first draft out. | ...runs it, and replies or checks their sentence (Emails 1 to 4). |
| 3. Emails | Activation, not selling. Branches: stuck gets the setup fix, generic output gets niche-tuning, ran it gets the workshop waitlist. | ...replies "held", "workshop" or names a real need. |
| 4. Workshop (waitlist) | Live: bring one idea, tune the four agents to the niche, review outputs together. Waitlist by reply until a page and date exist. Paid, not yet priced. | ...asks for more than a group can give. |
| 5. Done-with-you (reply only) | A team set up around their offer, reviewed on their first outputs. A scoped conversation, never advertised. | End of ladder. |

Rules for the ladder:
- Rungs 1 to 3 are free and complete on their own. Nothing is withheld from the folder to create an upsell.
