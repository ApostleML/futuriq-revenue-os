## Offer Architecture

**Who it is for:** Independent coaches and consultants who sell their time (1:1 or cohort), have real expertise, and no written, productized offer. Creators and other entrepreneurs can use the kit, but the title, hook and CTA never address them.

**Painful problem:** They have used AI to make a lead magnet, content and emails. Each piece was fine. Side by side they describe three different businesses, so a visitor who likes them still cannot tell what they sell, and does not join the list. (Pain framing is Inference, consistent with the market-signal finding that people fear "AI slop" and want one clear thing to build first.)

**Measurable outcome:** One sitting, one idea in, a first-draft go-to-market kit out. The check is binary: the same offer sentence appears word for word in the content angles, the lead magnet and email 1 (the Hand-Off Check). It is a draft to edit, not a launched business. No income claim.

**Why now:** Agent-team content is current (Claude Code agent teams are a 2026 feature) and the market is crowded with sales-pitch videos. Viewers are primed to distrust them, so inspectable proof is the opening.

## Unique Mechanism: The Revenue Chain

| Order | Agent | Job | Contract |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | market-signal-researcher | Demand, buyer psychology, opportunity score | Research only; labels inference |
| 2 | offer-architect | Positioning and offer | Produces ONE offer sentence, at the top of its file |
| 3 | content-angle-strategist | Title, hook, angles, retention map | Quotes the offer sentence word for word; flags it if missing |
| 4 | conversion-system-builder | Lead magnet, CTA, 5 emails | Quotes the offer sentence word for word; flags it if missing |
| - | Coordinator | Runs the order, rejects weak output, assembles the kit | Rejects any output that drops the sentence |

**Why a chain beats one long prompt:** a single prompt has no hand-off you can inspect, so drift is invisible. In the chain you search for the sentence. If it is missing, you know which agent dropped it and fix that one file.
