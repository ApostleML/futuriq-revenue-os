## Market Signal Brief (run-2)

Inputs read: business-brief.md and runbooks/revenue-agent-runbook.md only. Nothing in outputs/ was read. Web research was two light searches. Evidence is labeled **[PUBLIC]** (seen in search results this run), **[BRIEF]** (stated in business-brief.md) or **[INFERENCE]** (my reasoning, not verified). I did not find search-volume, CPM or conversion data. Treat every demand claim marked INFERENCE as a hypothesis to test.

### Market category
AI-for-business-owners education that leads to a systems or template offer. Specifically: "agentic workflows for solo experts", sold to coaches, consultants, creators and service providers. Claude Code is the vehicle. The real product is a repeatable revenue pipeline: idea, positioning, offer, content, lead magnet, follow-up.

### Demand Pattern

**Public evidence**
- **[PUBLIC]** Claude Code subagents are a documented, official feature: markdown files with YAML frontmatter, each with its own context window, system prompt and tool access. They can be created with `/agents`, and delegation is driven by the agent's description (docs at code.claude.com and docs.anthropic.com). The concept is real and teachable.
- **[PUBLIC]** Anthropic's blog has a post on "how and when to use subagents in Claude Code" ([claude.com/blog](https://claude.com/blog/how-and-when-to-use-subagents-in-claude-code)). There is also a partner course on Skilljar. Interest is strong enough for the vendor to invest in educating on it.
- **[PUBLIC]** The existing subagent tutorials I found are developer-oriented: a freeCodeCamp/ExamPro course of about 12 hours, a ZazenCodes Substack walkthrough building product-manager and software-engineer agents, and the Educative course. The search summary said it found no tutorials aimed at coaches or consultants. This is a gap signal, but it comes from one search and is not proof.
- **[PUBLIC]** A paid Maven course ("How To Build With Claude Code Sub-Agents") targets professionals and business owners and reportedly has about 124 students. This suggests some willingness to pay for a business-owner framing of subagents, at small scale. Treat it as a weak signal because the enrollment figure came from a search summary.
- **[PUBLIC]** Some results were spammy aggregator pages listing YouTube videos. That suggests the keyword is attracting low-quality supply (inference from that observation).

**From the brief**
- **[BRIEF]** The audience "knows AI is important but is overwhelmed by tools and doesn't know what business system to build first." The brief asserts this pain. I have not verified it with public data.

**Inference**
- **[INFERENCE]** Searchable phrases probably include "Claude Code for business", "Claude Code subagents", "AI agents for coaches", "AI business system", "AI content workflow for YouTube", "AI offer creation" and "lead magnet with AI". I did not confirm search volumes.
- **[INFERENCE]** "Claude Code" has a developer image. Non-technical entrepreneurs may not search for it by name. They search by outcome ("AI to build my offer", "AI to get clients"). The title therefore has to carry the outcome and put Claude Code in the supporting role.
- **[INFERENCE]** "AI agent team" and "systems not tools" fit a current shift from "which tool" to "which workflow". The brief claims overwhelm, which is consistent with this, but I have no data on its size.

### Audience Psychology

**Sophistication:** Mixed and skeptical-intermediate. They have used ChatGPT or Claude for chat and have seen many "AI tools" videos. They are not developers. The brief says "beginner-friendly without basic," so the video must be simple to follow but should not repeat the prompt-engineering content they already distrust. **[INFERENCE]**

- **Want:** A clear first system to build that produces offers, leads and revenue assets. They want to stop hopping between tools and to see one repeatable thing they could copy this week. **[BRIEF + INFERENCE]**
- **Fear:** Falling behind, wasting time and money on the wrong stack, and sounding generic. Many also fear that the terminal or code environment is "not for me." **[INFERENCE]**
- **Misunderstand:** They believe the next tool or a better prompt is the leverage. The brief reframes this as "the money is in systems, not tools." They also likely think "agents" means a developer-only or highly technical setup, when a subagent here is a markdown file. **[INFERENCE, supported by the PUBLIC fact about the file format]**
- **Secretly hope:** That a small set of agents can do their positioning, content and follow-up work while they stay the expert. They hope someone will hand them a template so they do not have to design the system. This is the opening for the Revenue Agent template download. **[INFERENCE]**

### Risk
What would make this feel generic or unbelievable:
1. **Generic output.** If the agents' positioning, offer and lead magnet read like standard AI copy ("Unlock your potential"), the "money is in systems" claim collapses. The demo needs one narrow, concrete business idea and outputs a viewer could imagine paying for.
2. **Tool tutorial drift.** Spending time on installing or configuring Claude Code turns it into the generic tool tutorial the brief forbids.
3. **Unproven revenue claim.** "Creates leverage, leads and revenue" with no case or number reads as hype. Do not invent results. Show the system running, not income claims.
4. **Jargon wall.** "Subagents", "context windows" and "YAML" without a one-line plain-English translation will lose the non-technical audience.
5. **Template as a bait-and-switch.** If the downloadable does not match what is shown on screen, trust is lost.
6. **Crowding.** Developer tutorials are plentiful, but the business-owner version is also being sold in paid courses, so differentiation has to come from the revenue pipeline and output quality, not from the subagent feature itself.

### Strategic Recommendation
- **Lead with the outcome, keep Claude Code as the method.** Frame it as "one idea in, a full revenue system out", with the agent team as the how. This serves both people who search by outcome and those who search "Claude Code subagents."
- **Show the handoff chain with one concrete, narrow business idea.** Each agent has one job and passes a clear artifact to the next. Use real output on screen, and pick an example whose output is visibly specific. This is the credibility proof and the answer to "not generic."
- **Make "the money is in systems, not tools" the spine.** Explain once in plain language that a subagent is a role file, then spend the time on the pipeline from idea to follow-up and why separating roles beats one big prompt. Skip installation and prompt basics.
- **Position the Revenue Agent template as the natural next step.** The viewer sees the system run, then gets the files so they do not have to rebuild it. Keep workshop and paid systems as the next rung and match the download exactly to what was shown.
- **Validate before finalizing.** Check actual search terms and the top competing videos for "Claude Code subagents" and for outcome phrases such as "AI offer creation", since volume and competition are unverified here. Do not make income or results claims that cannot be shown on screen.
