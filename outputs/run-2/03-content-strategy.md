## Content Strategy

**Offer sentence (quoted word for word from 02-offer-architecture.md):**
"I help coaches and consultants who sell their time turn what they already sell into one clear offer, with content, lead magnet and emails that all say it, using a four-agent Revenue Chain."

### Best Title
**Turn Your Coaching Into One Clear Offer With 4 AI Agents**

Why it wins:
- Understood in 3 seconds: buyer (coaching), outcome (one clear offer), method (4 AI agents).
- Outcome-led, no "get rich", no income claim. It names a buyer, where the competing "If You Want to Get Rich With AI, Build This 4-Agent Claude Team First" is broad and money-led.
- It compresses the offer sentence ("turn what they already sell into one clear offer ... four-agent Revenue Chain") into a title without over-explaining. "Claude Code" stays out of the title; the tool is proof, not the promise.

Angle pattern note: #3 and #5 use "The [system] that creates [outcome]"; #1 uses "I built [specific system] so you can copy it"; #4 uses "Stop using [tool] like this".
