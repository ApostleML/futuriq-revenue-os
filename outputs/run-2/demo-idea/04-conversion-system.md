## Conversion System: First Account Manager Handoff

Labels: [BRIEF] stated in the brief. [INFERENCE] guess, unverified. [HYPOTHESIS] offer or price guess, to be tested. No results, testimonials or personal claims are invented. Placeholders in [SQUARE BRACKETS] are filled by the creator with real details before filming or sending.

### Lead Magnet

**Name:** The Handoff-First Starter: Role Scorecard + Client Handoff Checklist

**Promise:** "I help owners of 2-8 person marketing or creative agencies hire and onboard their first account manager without a costly bad hire or clients feeling handed off, by building a role scorecard and handoff plan before the hire." This download is the blank version of the first two documents from the video, so you can build yours today.

**Contents** (the same documents built on screen in the video, as editable templates):
1. Role scorecard, blank. Sections: what the account manager owns, what stays with the owner, what a good first 90 days looks like, and candidate criteria rows for a pass/fail check.
2. Client handoff checklist, blank. One row per client: who owns what, what the client expects, and how you introduce the new person, with a space for the intro wording.
3. The filled-in EXAMPLE versions from the video (labelled EXAMPLE), so the owner can see a finished one next to their blank.

Not included on purpose: the first-30-days plan template. The video shows it, but the full 30-day plan sits in the template pack (see Sales Path). [HYPOTHESIS] Keeping it back gives the pack a reason to exist; if viewers ask for it, move it into the free download.

**Why someone wants it now:**
- The video ends with the documents half-understood and the owner's own clients still unmapped. The download lets them do their own version while the build is fresh.
- It is the work the video says the owner has to do first. [INFERENCE] An owner who is the bottleneck today has no reason to wait.
- It is the actual file from the screen, not a summary of the video.

### Follow-Up Sequence

Sender: [Coach name]. Voice: direct, practical. No income claims, no results, no stories unless real. Send timing is a suggestion. [HYPOTHESIS]

**Email 1 (immediately): Your scorecard and handoff checklist**
- Core idea: deliver the file, restate what it is for, give one small first action.
- Subject: Your role scorecard and handoff checklist
- Body:
  Here are the files from the video: [DOWNLOAD LINK]
  Inside: the blank role scorecard, the blank client handoff checklist, and the filled-in examples.
  Here is what I do: "I help owners of 2-8 person marketing or creative agencies hire and onboard their first account manager without a costly bad hire or clients feeling handed off, by building a role scorecard and handoff plan before the hire."
  Start with one box only: the "stays with the owner" column on the scorecard. Write down what you will still do after the hire. This is the part most owners skip. [INFERENCE]
  This takes real work up front. The file does not hire anyone for you. It makes the role clear before you recruit.
- CTA: Open the scorecard and fill in the "stays with the owner" column.

**Email 2 (day 2): The column that decides the hire**
- Core idea: the letting-go step is the one that matters most. [INFERENCE] Ties to the scorecard.
- Subject: What you keep vs. what you hand over
- Body:
  A role scorecard fails when the "owns" column is full and the "stays with the owner" column is empty. [INFERENCE] The new hire then inherits tasks you have not actually let go of.
  Today: fill the "owns" column. Be specific: tasks, not titles. "Weekly client status update" is a task. "Client communication" is not.
  Then look at your "stays with the owner" column. If it is shorter than you expected, check it again.
  If you got stuck, reply and tell me which column. I read the replies.
- CTA: Finish the "owns" and "stays with the owner" columns.

**Email 3 (day 4): Judge candidates against the page, not the gut**
- Core idea: the candidate criteria rows turn the bad-hire worry into a written check. No promise that a bad hire is impossible.
- Subject: Check candidates against the role
- Body:
  A written role gives you something to hold a candidate up against. In the video I use one criteria row as a pass/fail check. Use the same rows in your template.
  Write each criterion so a candidate either shows it or does not. "Good with clients" is not checkable. "Can describe how they ran a client check-in and what they did when it went badly" is.
  A scorecard will not make a bad hire impossible. It lets you see the mismatch sooner, and it gives you written reasons for your decision.
  Affordability is a separate question. The scorecard makes the scope explicit so you can judge the cost.
- CTA: Rewrite your three vaguest criteria so each one is checkable.

**Email 4 (day 6): Hand off one client first**
- Core idea: do the handoff client by client. Introduces the first-30-days plan.
- Subject: Map one client before you map them all
- Body:
  Clients tend to say they want to talk to you, not the new person. [INFERENCE] Handle it one client at a time.
  Open the handoff checklist and fill in one row: who owns what, what this client expects, and how you will introduce the new person. Write the actual wording of the introduction.
  Do that for one client. If you can't write the intro, that tells you something about the role or the client. Fix it before you hire.
  The video also shows a first-30-days plan, with check-ins in weeks 1 and 2 so you can see early if it is working. That plan comes after the scorecard and the handoff map. I'll cover where to get it in the next email.
- CTA: Fill in one client row on the handoff checklist.

**Email 5 (day 8): If you want help applying it**
- Core idea: a plain, one-time offer of the next step. No pressure. Ties back to the same system.
- Subject: Want help with your own version?
- Body:
  Over the past week the free files covered two of the three documents: the role scorecard and the client handoff map. The third is the first-30-days plan.
  Two options if you want more than the free files:
  1. [HYPOTHESIS] A template pack: the scorecard, handoff map, first-30-days plan and interview questions, ready to edit. [LINK, only once the pack exists]
  2. A short coaching program where we apply this to your clients, your hire and the onboarding. [HYPOTHESIS] 1:1, about 4-6 weeks. [Link to a call-booking or interest form, only once the program is defined]
  If you are not ready, keep the free files. They work without me.
  If you want to talk through whether the program fits your agency, reply "handoff" and I'll tell you what it covers and whether I think it fits.
- CTA: Reply "handoff", or use the link for the option that fits.

4. Email 5 links to the template pack and program. Do not send it with dead links. If the pack or program does not exist yet, use only the "reply handoff" option.

8. Email 1 states the offer sentence in full. It is long. Keep it as a quote and do not shorten it in this email, or the contract with the video and the lead magnet breaks.
9. The example scorecard and client rows must stay labelled EXAMPLE in the download. No invented results, client names or testimonials.
