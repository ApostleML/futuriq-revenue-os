## Market Signal Brief: Coaching for first-time agency owners hiring their first account manager

Method note: I read only `outputs/run-2/demo-idea/business-brief.md`. I did no live research, so there are no [PUBLIC] claims below. Everything is [BRIEF] or [INFERENCE]. Before filming, check the search-demand claims in YouTube autocomplete and Google.

### Demand Pattern
- [BRIEF] The audience is owners of 2-8 person marketing or creative agencies. They still handle every client relationship themselves and have never hired an account manager.
- [BRIEF] The offer is a short coaching program that takes the owner from "I do all client work" to a hired, onboarded first account manager. Leads come from YouTube, then email.
- [INFERENCE] People in this spot probably search for a mix of phrases:
  - "when to hire an account manager"
  - "agency account manager job description"
  - "how to stop being the bottleneck in my agency"
  - "how to delegate client communication"
- [INFERENCE] The trigger is likely that the owner can't take on new clients or take time off without client relationships suffering. Hiring becomes the obvious fix, and then the owner doesn't know how to do it.
- [INFERENCE] The search intent is "how do I do this step" (role definition, hiring, handoff, onboarding), not "should I". That suits a tutorial or process video.

### Audience Psychology
- Wants: [INFERENCE] Out of day-to-day client management, without clients leaving or quality dropping.
- Fears: [INFERENCE]
  - A bad first hire. In a small team the salary is a large share of costs, and one mistake is expensive.
  - Clients saying "I want to talk to you, not them".
  - Being unable to afford the hire.
- Misunderstands: [INFERENCE] They likely treat this as a recruiting problem (where to find someone). It is more likely a handoff problem: the role, client expectations and the owner's own letting go are undefined before the hire.
- Secretly hopes: [INFERENCE] That a hire will take the whole client burden off them with little effort on their side.
- Sophistication: [INFERENCE] Intermediate on running an agency, beginner on managing a person in this role. They are skeptical of generic "scale your agency" content.

### Risk
- [INFERENCE] It sounds like generic "delegate and scale" advice. A tight niche does not make a video specific unless it shows an actual artifact.
- [BRIEF] No income claims and no invented results or testimonials. The video cannot lean on "I grew my agency by X", so it has to prove value by showing the method.
- [BRIEF] The coach is solo and the identity is "example only". Any personal story must be real, not made up.
- [INFERENCE] Any demand or search-volume number stated without a source would be unbelievable. Keep guesses labeled as guesses, as the brief requires.

### Strategic Recommendation
- Frame the video around one concrete handoff problem, such as the role and handoff before the hire. Show it as a working artifact (scorecard, checklist or 30-day plan) on screen. [INFERENCE]
- Title around the step, not the dream: "how to hire", "what to hand over first". Avoid growth or scale promises. [INFERENCE]
- Sell proof through process: show the actual document being built, with no results claims. [BRIEF rules]
- Send the viewer to a free template of that artifact via email, and keep the coaching program as the next step after it. [BRIEF: YouTube, then email]
