# Business Brief (run-2 demo idea rehearsal)

Copied from Section A of starter-folder/business-brief.md, the worked example shown in the video.

Business idea:
A coaching program that helps first-time agency owners hire their first account manager.

Who I am:
A solo coach who has run a small agency and hired account managers. (Example only.)

Audience:
Owners of small marketing or creative agencies (roughly 2 to 8 people) who still handle every client relationship themselves and have never hired an account manager.

What I sell or want to sell:
A short coaching program that takes the owner from "I do all client work" to a hired, onboarded first account manager.

Where I want leads from:
YouTube, then email.

Voice:
Direct, practical, no fluff.

Rules:
- No income claims and no invented results or testimonials.
- Label guesses as guesses.
