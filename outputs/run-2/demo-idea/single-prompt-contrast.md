# First Account Manager Coaching: Offer, Video, Lead Magnet, Email 1

## 1. One-sentence offer

A short coaching program for owners of 2 to 8 person agencies who still run every client relationship themselves, taking you from "I do all the client work" to a hired, onboarded first account manager, with the role defined, the hire made and the handoff planned.

## 2. YouTube title and 20-second hook

**Title:** How to Hire Your First Account Manager (Without Losing Your Clients)

**20-second hook (about 55 words):**

> If every client email, call and "quick question" still comes to you, your agency can't grow past you. Hiring an account manager fixes that, but most first hires go wrong because the owner never wrote down what the job actually is. In this video I'll walk you through the exact steps I'd follow, from defining the role to the first 30 days.

## 3. Lead magnet

**Name:** The First Account Manager Hiring Kit

**What's inside:**
- A one-page role scorecard: what your account manager owns, what stays with you, and how you'll judge the first 90 days
- A client handoff checklist for moving each account from you to them without the client feeling dropped
- Ten interview questions that show how a candidate handles an unhappy client
- A 30-day onboarding plan, week by week

**Promise line:** Everything you need to define, hire and hand off to your first account manager, so client work stops depending on you.

## 4. Email 1 of the follow-up sequence

**Subject:** Your hiring kit (start with page 1)

**Preview text:** The scorecard is the part most owners skip.

Hi {first_name},

Here's your First Account Manager Hiring Kit: [download link]

One suggestion before you dive in: start with the role scorecard on page 1, not the interview questions.

Most owners I've talked to want to jump straight to finding candidates. But if you can't write down what the account manager owns and what you keep, you'll end up hiring someone and then doing their job for them, which is the thing you were trying to stop.

So here's your task for this week. Block 20 minutes and fill in the scorecard. Don't make it perfect. Just list:

1. The client tasks you do every week
2. Which of those you'd hand over first
3. What "doing it well" looks like after 90 days

That list is the start of your job description, your interview plan and your onboarding plan.

Tomorrow I'll send you the most common mistake I see in the handoff, and how to avoid it.

Reply and tell me: how many active clients are you managing yourself right now? I read every reply.

[Your name]

P.S. If you'd rather not work through this alone, I run a short coaching program that takes you from doing all the client work to a hired, onboarded account manager. More on that later this week.
