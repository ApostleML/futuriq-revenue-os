## Market Signal Brief

**Evidence note:** 2 of 4 allowed searches run. Cited items name a URL. Everything else is labeled (Inference). I found no hard search-volume or view-count data, and I invented none.

### Demand Pattern
**Public evidence:**
- Claude Code subagents are an active tutorial topic, but the content is developer-oriented. Examples are a product manager, engineer and test-runner agent demo (https://zazencodes.substack.com/p/claude-code-sub-agents-tutorial), a DataCamp subagents course (https://www.datacamp.com/courses/introduction-to-subagents) and an Educative course (https://www.educative.io/courses/claude-code/sub-agents-and-internal-agent-orchestration).
- Business-owner framing exists but is thin and promotional. One blog pitches Claude Code agents running business operations, with one YouTube-analytics-report example and an unsupported "80-90% of routine operations" claim (https://growwstacks.com/blog/claude-code-for-business-run-90-percent-with-ai-agents/).
- Coach and consultant guidance centers on "start with one use case or bottleneck, not a whole system" (https://mindfulagile.substack.com/p/5-beginner-questions-high-performing, https://circle.so/blog/ai-tools-for-coaches). It is mostly tool roundups and session-notes use cases.
- A paid workshop promised "concrete, repeatable AI workflows you can use the next day" for coaches and consultants (https://raleighnc.gov/doing-business/events/partner-event-become-ai-powered-coach-or-consultant-90-minutes-online).

**Inference:** The gap is the intersection. Developer content covers subagents but not revenue. Coach content covers tool lists and admin tasks but not an agent system that produces positioning, offer, content and follow-up. The audience is looking for a "where do I start" answer. The "tools vs systems" framing matches the one-use-case advice, but it is not yet shown as a packaged, revenue-linked build.

### Audience Psychology
- **Sophistication (Inference):** Beginner-to-intermediate with AI, but experienced in business. Likely mildly skeptical of "AI hype".
- **Wants (Inference):** A defined first system, proof it makes money, and something they can copy today.
- **Fears (Inference):** Choosing the wrong tool, wasting weeks, and looking behind. Also fear of terminal and code tools. Claude Code sounds technical to non-developers.
- **Misunderstands (Inference):** That more tools or better prompts equal leverage. Also that agents are only for developers.
- **Secretly hopes (Inference):** That one setup can do the marketing thinking for them and a template means they don't have to learn it.

### Risk
- **Generic feel (Inference):** A "5 agents, here's the output" demo looks like every other agent-hype video. Vague outputs ("a positioning statement") would make it feel generic.
- **Unbelievable (Inference):** Claiming the agent team produces revenue without showing a real input idea and real, specific outputs. The 80-90% style claims in promotional content (growwstacks link) show the kind of overreach viewers already distrust.
- **Too technical:** Showing config files without explaining why would lose the beginner half of the audience.
- **Too much:** Five deliverables in under 15 minutes may become a blur.

### Strategic Recommendation
- **Open on the outcome.** Start with the finished output for a real, specific business idea (offer, hook list, lead magnet, email 1), then reveal that one agent team built it.
- **Make "systems, not tools" the spine.** Frame it as the answer to the one-bottleneck, one-system guidance beginners already hear. Show why a chained pipeline beats five separate prompts.
- **Cut to 3 visible agent hand-offs.** Offer, content angles, lead magnet plus follow-up. Show the hand-off between agents as the proof of a system.
- **De-risk Claude Code.** Give a 30-second "no coding needed" bridge, and use the downloadable Revenue Agent template as the CTA. Keep the workshop as the step after the template.
- **Title and thumbnail test.** Lead with the business result, not "Claude Code". Treat the title as a hypothesis, since search volume is unverified.
