## 3. Why This Topic Should Work

- **The gap is real (Evidence + Inference).** Claude Code subagent content today is built for developers (DataCamp, Educative, Zazen Codes). Coach and consultant AI content is mostly tool roundups. Nobody is showing business owners a connected agent system that produces revenue assets.
- **It answers the pain they already have (Evidence).** Coach-focused sources repeat "start with one bottleneck, one system". This video is that system.
- **It is demonstrable (Inference).** Real output on screen, with a clock running, can be checked. That's what separates it from "AI will run 90% of your business" content, which viewers already distrust.
- **It monetizes naturally.** Every output is a revenue asset, and the template is the obvious next step.

## 5. The Unique Mechanism: The Revenue Chain

Four specialists and a coordinator. Each agent reads the previous agent's file, so nothing is written in isolation.

1. **market-signal-researcher**: demand, buyer psychology, opportunity score. Research only, never strategy.
2. **offer-architect**: positioning, offer, value ladder, objections. Writes the **offer sentence**, the thread for everything after it.
3. **content-angle-strategist**: titles, hook and retention map, all built on that offer sentence.
4. **conversion-system-builder**: lead magnet, CTA and emails, all quoting that offer sentence.
5. **Coordinator** (the main session): runs them in order, rejects weak output, assembles the final kit.

**Why it beats prompts:** separate prompts lose the offer by step three. The chain carries it through, and the viewer can see it happen in the highlighted hand-offs.

## 9. 5-Email Follow-Up Sequence

Trigger: kit download. Every email includes an unsubscribe link and a sender address.

**Email 1 (immediately): Your Revenue Chain template is inside**
Here's your kit: [link]. Do three things today. Follow the ten-minute setup guide. Fill in the Intake Sheet with ONE real idea and a named audience. Run the chain. Then do the check that matters: find your offer sentence in the angles, the lead magnet and email 1. If it's there, the chain works. If it's missing, hit reply and tell me where it dropped. You'll get a strong first draft, not a finished business. Draft first, edit second.
*CTA: Run it on one idea today.*

**Email 2 (day 1): Run it on one idea, not ten**
People stall hunting for the perfect idea. Pick the one you could sell to a named person this month, like "first-time engineering managers." A named niche beats a broad one every time. Run the chain once, start to finish, before you edit a word. Your first run is your baseline. Stuck? Reply with your idea in one sentence and I'll tell you what to tighten.
*CTA: Run your first pass, or reply with your idea.*

**Email 3 (day 3): Make it sound like you**
AI drafts stay generic until you edit the right three things, in this order: your offer sentence (everything downstream reads it), your email 1 subject line, and one proof point only you can tell. Change the top of the chain and the chain carries it down. Twenty minutes on these three beats rewriting everything.
*CTA: Make the three edits and rerun the agents downstream.*

**Email 4 (day 5): When the chain breaks (and how to fix it)**
The common breaks are a fuzzy intake sheet, an offer sentence that's too long, or agents run out of order. Fix the input, rerun, and watch the sentence travel again. If you want me over your shoulder for your first build, the Revenue Chain Workshop is 90 minutes live: you bring your idea, we build your chain, and Q&A is included. It's optional. Plenty of people do fine with the kit alone.
*CTA: See the Workshop details (optional).*

**Email 5 (day 8): Want this built into your business?**
If you see the value but don't want to build and maintain it yourself, there's the Revenue System Install. We build the chain around your offer, tune it to your voice, and add one extension agent such as sales-call prep or proposals. It's two working sessions plus 30 days of support. No results are promised, because they depend on your offer and your follow-through. If it fits, book a short call. If not, keep the kit. It's yours.
*CTA: Book a no-obligation fit call.*

**Segmenting:** anyone who clicked the Workshop link in Email 4 gets the workshop reminder. Anyone who clicked or replied to Email 5 gets a personal reply. Everyone else stays on the newsletter.

## 11. What to Remove Because It Feels Generic

- Any "AI saves you hours" or "work smarter" phrasing. Say what gets produced instead.
- Tool roundups, model comparisons, "my favorite AI tools" segments.
- Prompt-engineering tips. This is a systems video, not a prompting lesson.
- Explaining what an LLM or an agent is in theory. Show it working instead.
- Income screenshots, "6-figure" language, countdown timers, fake limited seats.
- Pitching the Install inside the video. That lives in Email 5 only.
- Any "AI runs your whole business" or "80–90% automated" claims. Viewers already distrust them.
- Long terminal or config walkthroughs. Five seconds of a file plus one sentence on why it exists is enough.
