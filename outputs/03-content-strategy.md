## Content Strategy

**Example idea used on screen:** A leadership coaching program for first-time engineering managers. Second idea (cut-in): a pricing consultant for wedding photographers.
**Framework name:** The Revenue Chain. **On-screen agents (real names):** market-signal-researcher, offer-architect, content-angle-strategist, conversion-system-builder, run by a coordinator per `runbooks/revenue-agent-runbook.md`.

### Best Title
**I Built an AI Team That Turns 1 Idea Into a Sellable Offer**

Why it wins:
- Understood in 3 seconds: one idea in, an offer out.
- Leads with the business result. "Claude Code" stays out of the title, which avoids filtering out non-technical coaches and consultants (market-signal risk).
- Uses the "I built [specific system] so you can copy it" pattern, which matches the free template CTA.
- "Sellable" signals revenue without promising income.
- "AI Team" is plain language for what the audience would otherwise call "subagents".

### Hook
First 20 seconds, word for word. Screen: the finished kit for the engineering-manager coach, scrolling fast.

"This is a complete offer, ten video angles, a lead magnet, and a five-email follow-up. For a leadership coach who serves first-time engineering managers. It started as one sentence. No strategist. No five tabs of prompts. Four agents built this, each one reading the last one's work. In the next fourteen minutes, I'll build it live, and then you get the template free."

### Screen Recording Beats
1. **0:00** Full-screen scroll of the finished kit. Hard cuts, no talking head.
2. **0:35** Zoom on the positioning sentence. Yellow highlight. Keep this style for every later hand-off.
3. **1:45** Split screen: five separate prompts, offer forgotten by step three, against the chain carrying it through.
4. **2:35** Slow, zoomed clicks for the no-coding bridge. Cursor ring on each click. Text overlay: "No code. Paste. Run."
5. **3:15** Type the idea character by character. Pin a running clock to the corner.
6. **4:10** Show the agent starting in the terminal. Zoom on the agent name `market-signal-researcher` as it runs.
7. **4:40** Quick cut to the raw agent file for 5 seconds. Overlay: "Plain-English instructions. That's all."
8. **5:45** Zoom on the offer-architect output. Freeze on the positioning sentence.
9. **7:15** Hand-off 1: animate an arrow from the offer sentence into angle #1. Highlight both.
10. **8:00** Point at one weak angle, delete it, rerun that line. This is the "real build" beat.
11. **9:30** Hand-off 2: highlight the offer sentence inside the lead magnet outline.
12. **10:30** Hand-off 3: highlight it again in email 1. Zoom hard, hold 2 seconds.
13. **11:15** Before/after edit on one email subject line. Side by side.
14. **12:10** Second idea runs at 4x speed with the clock visible. Show the final file list.
15. **13:15** Show the template download page and the link in the description. Do not show income screenshots at any point.
