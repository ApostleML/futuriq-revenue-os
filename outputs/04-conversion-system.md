### Sales Path

1. **Video** (free): shows the Revenue Chain working on a real idea, with the offer sentence handed off 3 times.
2. **Starter Kit** (free, email): they run it on their own idea. Success is the Hand-Off Check passing. This is the proof step, and it builds trust.
3. **Revenue Chain Workshop** (suggested $97-$197, a price to test): for people who ran the template and want guided building. Offered once in the video and in Email 4.
4. **Revenue System Install** (suggested $1,500-$3,500, a price to test): done-with-you build plus one extension agent. Offered only inside the email sequence (Email 5), via a fit call. Not pitched in the video.

Segment on behavior: clicked the workshop link in Email 4 gets the workshop reminder; clicked or replied to Email 5 gets a personal reply and a fit call. Everyone else stays on the template and gets future videos.

### Friction Check

1. **"Claude Code is for developers" fear.** Setup is the biggest drop-off risk. Mitigation: the walkthrough must be click-by-click and tested by a non-technical person before launch, and Email 1 should invite replies for help.
2. **The template must work first time.** If the first run fails, trust is gone. Test the download on a clean machine and a clean project before publishing, and include the worked example so output can be compared.
3. **Pitch creep.** The video mentions the workshop once and never the install. Email 5 is the first mention of the install. Keep the workshop and install out of Emails 1-3.
4. **Opt-in clarity.** The page must say plainly: free, email required, optional workshop later. Hidden catches will read as forced.
5. **Link overload.** One primary link (the template) in the description and pinned comment, with the workshop secondary. Two equal links split attention.
6. **Claims discipline.** Never use income claims, testimonials not yet earned, countdown timers or fake limited seats. Workshop dates must be real. Say "strong first draft" everywhere.
7. **Pricing is unvalidated.** The workshop and install prices are guesses with no price data behind them. Test with a small cohort before treating them as fixed.
8. **Name consistency.** Use only the four real agent names on all pages and files, never the retired "Positioner/Angler/Magnet Builder/Follower-Upper" names.
9. **Compliance.** Include unsubscribe and sender address in every email, and get consent at opt-in.
