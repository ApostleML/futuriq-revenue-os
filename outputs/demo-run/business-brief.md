# Business Brief (demo-run)

Coordinator note: this brief was written for the demo run from a one-line idea. Anything not in that idea is a working assumption carried over from the FuturIQ brief format, and is marked [ASSUMPTION].

Business idea:
A leadership coaching program for first-time engineering managers.

Creator:
A solo leadership coach (unnamed) who sells this program. [ASSUMPTION]

Audience:
Software engineers who were recently promoted to their first engineering manager role, or are about to be. [ASSUMPTION: also their employers, who may pay for coaching]

Core promise:
To be defined by the offer-architect. Do not invent results or credentials for the coach.

Video goal: [ASSUMPTION, same as the FuturIQ brief]
Create a sub-15-minute YouTube video that feels practical and immediately useful to the audience.

Offer path: [ASSUMPTION]
The video should lead naturally to a free lead magnet, then to the paid coaching program.

Voice: [ASSUMPTION]
Direct, practical, credible to engineers, no fluff.

Constraints:
- No invented testimonials, results, income claims or coach credentials.
- Label inference as inference.
- Show the connection between the content and the paid program.
