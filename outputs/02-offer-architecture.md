## Offer Architecture

**Pricing note:** All prices below are suggestions to test, not evidence of what this audience will pay. The market-signal brief found one paid workshop for this audience (Raleigh URL) but no price data.

### Positioning
I help coaches, consultants, and service providers get a complete, launch-ready offer, content plan, lead magnet, and follow-up sequence from one business idea, without hiring a strategist or stitching together five disconnected AI prompts, by building a four-agent Claude Code team that hands work down a pipeline.

**Who it is for:** Coaches, consultants, creators, and service providers who are experienced in business, newer to AI, and stuck on "what system do I build first?"
**Current state (Inference):** A browser tab of tools, a few one-off prompts, and no connected output. They know AI matters and feel behind.
**Painful problem:** Tool overwhelm. Many tools and prompts, but no single system that turns an idea into something they can sell.

### Core Offer
**Name: The Revenue Agent Build**

A done-with-you way to install one working agent team that converts a single business idea into a sellable go-to-market kit.

**Measurable, honest outcome:** In one sitting, you go from one rough idea to a five-part kit: (1) a one-sentence positioning statement, (2) a named offer with price logic, (3) 10 YouTube angles, (4) one lead magnet, (5) a 5-email follow-up sequence. Success is "kit in hand, ready to publish". It is not a revenue claim. Revenue depends on the offer, the audience, and execution, and nothing here guarantees income.

**Concrete deliverables (template tier):**
- 4 pre-built agent files, ready to drop into a Claude Code project
- A one-page Idea Intake Sheet (the input the agents read)
- A worked example: one real idea in, full kit out
- A 10-minute setup walkthrough for non-developers (no coding)
- A "what to edit first" checklist for making the output sound like you

**Why now (Inference):** Claude Code subagents are being taught almost entirely to developers (DataCamp, Educative, Zazen Codes sources in 01-market-signal). Business-owner versions are thin and promotional. That window is open but will not stay open.

### Unique Mechanism
**The Revenue Chain** -- a four-agent pipeline in which each agent reads the previous agent's output, so nothing is written in isolation.

1. **Positioner:** reads the Idea Intake Sheet, outputs the positioning statement and the offer (using the formula: audience, outcome, without painful tradeoff, by building a system).
2. **Angler:** reads the offer, outputs 10 YouTube angles tied to the buyer's pain.
3. **Magnet Builder:** reads offer + angles, outputs the lead magnet outline and title.
4. **Follower-Upper:** reads everything above, outputs the follow-up email sequence that leads to the paid next step.

**Why it is believable:** The hand-offs are visible. The viewer sees the offer text reappear inside the angles, the magnet, and email 1. That consistency is the proof of a system, and five separate prompts cannot show it. It is also the "systems, not tools" spine: the value sits in the chain, not in any single model or app.

### Proof Requirements
What must appear on screen:
- A **real, specific starting idea** typed on screen (a named niche, not "a business"). No placeholders.
- **Actual agent outputs scrolling on screen**, unedited, including one mediocre line you then fix, to show it is real.
- The **visible hand-off:** highlight the offer sentence as it reappears in the angles, magnet, and email 1.
- The **raw agent files** shown for a few seconds, with a plain-English line on why they exist (avoids the "too technical" risk).
- A **clock or timestamp** on the run, so the speed claim is checkable.
- A **second, different idea** run through the same chain (short cut-in) to show the template is reusable, not a one-off.
- **Honest limits stated aloud:** "This gives you a strong first draft of your go-to-market kit. You still edit and test it. It does not guarantee sales."
- No income screenshots, and no "80-90% of operations" style claims (the market-signal brief flags these as already distrusted).

### Objection Map
| Objection | How the video addresses it |
|---|---|
| "Claude Code is for developers; I can't use a terminal." | A 30-second "no coding needed" bridge: open the project, paste the intake sheet, press one command. Show the exact clicks, and point to the setup walkthrough in the template. |
| "This looks like every other agent-hype video." | Open on the finished outputs for a real idea before explaining anything. Show hand-offs, not just results. |
| "AI output is generic; it won't sound like me." | Show the "what to edit first" step and one before/after edit. State that the output is a draft built from your intake answers. |
| "Can't I just use ChatGPT prompts?" | Contrast one separate prompt against the chain: separate prompts forget the offer by step three; the chain carries it through. |
| "Will this actually make me money?" | No income promise. Say the kit removes the blank-page work, and that sales still depend on your offer, audience, and follow-through. |
| "I'm overwhelmed; where do I even start?" | The system is the answer: one idea in, one kit out. "Start with one system, not ten tools." |
| "Is the template really free? What's the catch?" | Be upfront: free template for an email, a workshop is the optional next step, and no pressure sell inside the video. |
